# 15. Development Process

Cortex should communicate a clear delivery workflow.

## 01 --- Requirements

Understand the client's business, goals, audience, requirements,
references, and desired functionality.

## 02 --- Finalization

Finalize:

- requirements
- scope
- features
- content
- visual direction
- deliverables
- communication expectations

The project should not enter full development until the agreed
requirements and scope are finalized.

## 03 --- Design

Create the UI/UX direction and finalize the experience.

## 04 --- Development

Build the agreed website, application, software, or digital solution.

## 05 --- Testing & Refinement

Validate:

- functionality
- responsiveness
- usability
- performance
- compatibility
- agreed requirements

Fix required issues and refine the final implementation.

## 06 --- Deployment

Deploy the completed solution to the agreed production environment.

## 07 --- Handover

Provide the completed project and required handover materials.

The handover should include the agreed final deliverables and relevant
project access/source materials where applicable.

## 08 --- Support

Post-delivery maintenance, updates, improvements, and technical support
can be discussed based on the project requirements.

# 19. FAQ

## What services does Cortex provide?

Cortex provides web development, AI/ML, software development,
automation, cybersecurity, UI/UX and digital design, and data/analytics
solutions.

## How long does a typical project take?

The standard target for an agreed project is approximately **3 weeks**,
covering requirements finalization, design, development, testing,
deployment, and handover.

Complex projects may require a different timeline.

## How much does a project cost?

Cortex does not use a fixed public price for every project.

Pricing is discussed privately based on requirements, scope, complexity,
integrations, and timeline.

Use the **Get a Quote** or **Start a Conversation** CTA.

## Do you build websites for small businesses?

Yes. Cortex offers business websites for startups, small and medium
businesses, restaurants, hospitality, retail, professional services,
personal brands, and organizations.

## Can you build a custom web application?

Yes. Cortex can build custom web applications with frontend, backend,
APIs, databases, authentication, dashboards, and deployment.

## Do you provide AI/ML development?

Yes. Cortex works on AI applications, machine learning models,
recommendation systems, computer vision, generative AI, NLP, predictive
analytics, and AI automation.

## Can you work on an existing website or application?

Yes. Existing projects can be reviewed for improvements, redesign,
additional functionality, integrations, maintenance, or modernization.

## What happens after I submit the form?

The Cortex team reviews the submitted requirements and contacts the
client to understand the project, clarify requirements, and determine
the next steps.

## Do you provide deployment?

Yes. Deployment is included where it is part of the agreed project
scope.

## Do you provide the source code after completion?

Handover details, source-code access, hosting access, and other project
materials should follow the agreed project scope and finalization.

## Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance, updates, improvements, and technical support can be
discussed after delivery.
